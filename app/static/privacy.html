Privacy notice

Last updated: 6 November 2026

This notice explains how the personal finance service at finance.passi.cloud processes personal data.

Who operates the service

This is a privately operated, access-restricted personal expense analysis service. Questions and requests concerning personal data can be sent to admin@passi.cloud.

Data we process

  • Passi account identifiers and email address used for authentication;
  • bank account details made available with your consent, such as account name, IBAN, currency and balance-related metadata;
  • transaction details, including amount, currency, booking date, value date and payment description;
  • categories, budgets, account monitoring choices and other settings you create.

Purpose and legal basis

The data is processed to provide the expense analysis functionality you request. Bank data is accessed only after your explicit bank consent. The service does not sell personal data, use it for advertising, or initiate payments.

Services involved

Passi provides sign-in and supplies the verified account identity. Enable Banking provides the account-information connection to your bank. Your bank authenticates you and controls the consent flow. Data is disclosed to these providers only as required for authentication and bank-data access.

Storage and isolation

Application data is stored in PostgreSQL on the private service server. Each Passi user has a separate database schema. Browser, application and database access are restricted, and outbound application traffic is limited to the approved Passi and Enable Banking endpoints.

Retention

Bank authorisation lasts only for the period approved during the consent flow and must be renewed when it expires. Imported data and user settings are retained while the service is used. You may request deletion at any time; the associated imported bank data, categories and settings will then be removed, subject to any mandatory security or legal retention requirement.

Your choices and rights

You may stop monitoring an account, decline or revoke bank consent, and request access, correction, export or deletion of your stored data. To request deletion or exercise another data-protection right, email admin@passi.cloud.
